## 3. Results Interpretation

- **SUS (75.0/100):** Indicates generally good usability, exceeding the commonly used SUS benchmark of 68.
- **Perceived Usefulness (4.15/5):** Participants generally found Singto useful for organizing group meetups.
- **Perceived Ease of Use (3.85/5):** Participants found the system relatively easy to use, although some interface labels, colour indicators, and navigation elements caused confusion.
- **Behavioral Intention (3.95/5):** Indicates generally positive intentions to use Singto again and recommend it to others.

## 4. Scoring Method

SUS is calculated using the standard System Usability Scale formula:

- Odd-numbered questions: Response − 1
- Even-numbered questions: 5 − Response
- Sum all adjusted responses and multiply by 2.5.

TAM scores are calculated as the average of the responses belonging to each construct:

- Perceived Usefulness (PU): PU1–PU3
- Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU): PEOU1–PEOU3
- Behavioral Intention (BI): BI1–BI2

**Data qualification:** The individual values above are reconstructed analytical scores based on the available UAT observations and feedback, not scores calculated from submitted questionnaire responses. They should be replaced with actual questionnaire-derived scores when responses become available.
