## Responsibilities

I was primarily responsible for the UI/UX design of the Singto project. My responsibilities included conducting user surveys to gather information about users' needs and scheduling habits, as well as helping create User Personas to summarize user characteristics, behaviors, and pain points.

I also designed the user interface, created user flows, and developed interactive prototypes using Figma.

Additionally, I participated in user testing to evaluate the usability of the system and gather feedback from participants. I analyzed the feedback, identified areas for improvement, and redesigned several UI elements to make the interface clearer and easier to use. Once the updated designs were completed, I handed them over to my teammates for implementation.

## AI Disclosure

I used Stitch during the initial stage to explore the overall concept and visualize the system's workflow. I then redesigned and modified the initial ideas to better fit the project's requirements. I also used ChatGPT to brainstorm ideas and help formulate possible solutions to design problems. However, all final UI designs, design decisions, and prototyping work were created by me.

## Reflection

### 1. Lessons Learned

Using AI during app development has made the overall process much faster and more convenient, especially in terms of saving time. For example, after collecting information from multiple target users, AI helped us identify pain points more quickly. During the design process, AI also helped us visualize initial ideas and create rough concepts before developing the actual design. It also helped us implement the front end according to our designs.

Overall, I think AI has made the development process more efficient. However, we still need to contribute our own ideas and effort to achieve good results.

One thing that improved was the UI. The final interface was very close to our original design, so we barely needed to make additional changes. On the other hand, I think one thing that has become worse is my ability to generate ideas independently. Sometimes, it is difficult to start brainstorming on my own without first asking AI to suggest some ideas. I have become used to using AI as a starting point and then developing those ideas further myself.
