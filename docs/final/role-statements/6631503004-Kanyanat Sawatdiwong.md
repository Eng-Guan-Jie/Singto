**Role:** AI Lead

As the AI Lead of Singto, I was responsible for the scheduling recommendation logic that helps organizers identify suitable dates based on participant availability. My work focused on designing, implementing, and reviewing the Best Date functionality to ensure that its recommendations followed consistent and explainable rules.

The implemented solution uses deterministic, rule-based scheduling logic rather than a trained machine-learning model or generative AI model. It considers accepted participants, calculates availability counts for individual dates and consecutive date ranges, and identifies all candidate options with the highest availability. The system also supports a holiday filter based on weekends and configured fixed-date Thai public holidays.

I contributed to documenting the algorithm's processing workflow, recommendation rules, limitations, and proposed evaluation methodology. Evaluation focuses on availability calculation correctness, recommendation rule conformance, test case pass rate, and processing time.
